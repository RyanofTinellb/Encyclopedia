Choice

Choice is a fundamental force. Certain living beings can have an effect on the world around them simply by choosing to do so. This manifests in a number of ways. In most cases, an individual has negligible impact, but there is a greater effect when a group of people is choosing.

Choice is amplified when travelling through a wormhole: such a person can choose qualities, or a specific location.

Certain planets were blessed by the gods, that anyone born there will share an ability.

Magic

Magic is the ability to manipulate matter and energy on the physical plane. It is passed through the medium of touch.

Telepathy

A telepath can hear the thoughts of those around them. Telepathy spreads through both space and gingla: the additional four ginglaic dimensions imply that telepathic power drops off quickly as two people move further away.

Empathy

An empath can see the emotions of others. As empathy does not spread through gingla, it is stronger than telepathy to a greater distance, but cannot be used between two people with ginglaic separation. Thus Zhaladi and Fezhlê cannot sense each other so.

Session

People with this ability can sess the love another holds.
